Questions

What owners ask us

Will my customers know it's AI?

Yes. It introduces itself as your shop's virtual assistant and says the call may be recorded. Most callers care that someone picked up and they got on the schedule. The alternative is voicemail, and most people won't leave one.

What if it gets something wrong?

Emergencies run on fixed rules and go straight to your cell. It only offers open times from your calendar and only quotes prices you've approved. We read transcripts every week and adjust the setup. If anything on our end fails, the call rings through to you.

Do I need a new phone number?

No. You keep your number. We give you a line to forward calls to, either all of them or just after-hours and missed calls.

Does it work with my scheduling software?

It books into Google Calendar today, or into its own built-in schedule. Jobber and Housecall Pro are next on our list. If you use something else, tell us on the trial form.

What happens if I go over my call limit?

Calls ring straight through to you like they do today. Nothing gets cut off, and we'll talk about whether a bigger plan makes sense.

Is there a contract?

No. It's month to month and you can cancel anytime. You can also text PAUSE to switch it off for a day, or for the slow season.

Is recording calls legal where I am?

Every call opens with a recording notice, which is the standard approach. We store a text transcript, not audio. Talk to us if your state has specific rules you're worried about.
